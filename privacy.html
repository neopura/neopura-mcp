Privacy policy

Neopura MCP is a private tool used by one person, its owner, with his own Google account. It has no other users.

What Google data it accesses

The data of the owner's Google account that the owner grants through Google's consent screen: mail, Drive files, calendars, contacts, tasks, YouTube and the photos the tool itself uploads.

How it is used

Only to let the owner's own AI assistants read and act on the owner's data when he asks them to.

How it is stored

The Google authorization is kept encrypted on a server the owner runs. The server does not build a copy of the mailbox, the Drive or the rest of the account.

It keeps a journal of every request it makes: the parameters, with secrets removed and long content cut short, and the outcome. The journal can therefore contain short pieces of content, such as the text of a message the owner asked to send.

Files the owner asks it to download are written to a transfer folder on the same server and stay there until the owner deletes them.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with anyone. Content that the owner's AI assistants read is processed by the AI model provider the owner uses, under the owner's own account.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google account's security settings, under third-party connections.
